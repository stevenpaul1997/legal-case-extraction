                     SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT AND RELEASE

This Settlement Agreement is entered into as of May 1, 2026, between
MIRIAM OKONKWO ("Claimant") and HALVERSON LOGISTICS LLC ("Respondent"),
relating to Case No. CV-26-114892.

1. PAYMENT. Respondent shall pay Claimant the total sum of $150,000.00,
   payable as follows:
   (a) $50,000.00 within 30 days of the Effective Date;
   (b) $50,000.00 within 60 days of the Effective Date;
   (c) $50,000.00 within 90 days of the Effective Date.

2. DISMISSAL. Claimant shall file a dismissal with prejudice within 10
   business days of receipt of the final payment.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY. The parties shall not disclose the terms of this
   Agreement.

Effective Date: May 1, 2026
